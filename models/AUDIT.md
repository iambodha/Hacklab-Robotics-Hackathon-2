## What this means (written 22 Nov, from the table above)

**Timing is good enough for ACT and SmolVLA at 30 fps, as of the decimated recorder (`live-20s`).**
- State: 45 rows/s on the bus clock (50 Hz requested), and each motor is re-read at ~39 Hz. A 30 fps
  row's joint values are at most ~22 ms old (p95). That's under one 33 ms frame period.
- Cameras: 29.3 fps each, no dropped frames, arrival latency 3-6 ms. The two cameras are within
  1 ms of each other (p95), so one row's wrist and overview images show the same instant.
- One ~130 ms gap per camera in 19 s, on both cameras at once (a Station-side hiccup). The exporter
  samples causally, so such a gap repeats the previous frame for ~4 rows. Fine for training. For an
  episode with several gaps > 100 ms, the auto-labeller sets `keep: false`.
- The earlier `live-check` session (before the bus-clock decimation fix) had 1.2-1.4 s camera latency
  spikes and a 3.9 s state hole. Don't train on recordings from before commit 2b12182.

**Content is the blocker. No recording so far is a demonstration:**
1. **No action stream.** The leader arm wasn't connected and the follower was limp (torque 0, no
   commands), so `action` falls back to "next follower state", which is only a proxy. ACT and SmolVLA
   need the commanded action. Record with the leader on its bus (`5B61035034`; the recorder already
   records every bus) and teleop on, so `action = leader norm`. Or record executor runs with torque on,
   so `action = goal register`. The exporter picks whichever exists (`info.json elrobot.action_source`).
2. **No motion** (0 ticks of travel on every joint): these were static timing tests.
3. Two 20 s sessions at about 1.4 MB/s. 50 demos of 20 s at 30 fps would be about 1.4 GB on disk
   as recorded JPEG. With ~2.8 GB free on this Mac, record in batches and export at 30 fps (the
   exporter keeps the JPEGs, ~23 KB per frame per camera), or move the recordings off the laptop.

**Resolution.** 299x224 comes from Station's `resize_target: 224`. ACT (ResNet-18) is fine at this size.
SmolVLA resizes to 512x512 with padding, so it gets no more detail than we give it. If the policy
fails on fine detail (tissue corner, stain edge), raise `resize_target` before recording the demos
(it needs a Station restart, so the integrator decides).

**Mapping (part of the admission gate in POLICY_PLAN.md).** `observation.state` and `action` are both
Station's calibrated norm (0..1 per joint, gripper last, higher = open). A policy's output norm goes
back through the same calibrated ranges, so only the ranges frozen at recording time are valid.
Recordings carry `range_min/max` per row. Check they match the live ranges, or the SetupId changes.
